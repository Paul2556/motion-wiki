---
title: Glossary
---

The vocabulary you'll hear on your first day that nobody explains out loud. Quick lookups only:
each term links to the page that actually explains it in context.

| Term | Quick definition | Read more |
|---|---|---|
| Position paper | Written statement of your country's stance, submitted before the conference | [[before-the-conference\|Before the conference]] |
| Quorum | Minimum delegations present for the committee's decisions to count | [[committee-basics\|Committee basics]] |
| Present and voting | A roll-call response that forfeits your right to abstain on substantive votes | [[committee-basics\|Committee basics]], [[voting-procedure\|Voting procedure]] |
| GSL (General Speakers List) | The default speaking order committee falls back to outside caucuses | [[speeches-and-the-gsl\|Speeches and the GSL]] |
| Yield | What a speaker does with unused speech time, to the chair, to another delegate, or to questions | [[speeches-and-the-gsl\|Speeches and the GSL]] |
| Motion | A proposal to change what committee does next | [[motions\|Motions]] |
| Moderated caucus | Chair-controlled short-speech format on a specific sub-topic | [[motions\|Motions]], [[caucusing\|Caucusing]] |
| Unmoderated caucus | Free, unstructured negotiating time, where most real work happens | [[motions\|Motions]], [[caucusing\|Caucusing]] |
| Lobbying | The informal negotiating that fills unmoderated caucus time | [[caucusing\|Caucusing]] |
| Table (a resolution) | Means "set aside" in American usage, "bring forward" in British usage, so check which your conference uses | [[motions\|Motions]] |
| Point of order | Flags a procedural mistake by the chair; the only point that's real in the actual UN | [[points\|Points]] |
| Point of personal privilege | Raised for your own ability to participate (can't hear, room temperature, etc.) | [[points\|Points]] |
| Point of parliamentary inquiry | A question to the chair about how procedure works | [[points\|Points]] |
| Point of information | A question to another delegate about a speech they just gave | [[points\|Points]] |
| Working paper | Informal, pre-resolution draft with no format requirements yet | [[working-papers-and-resolutions\|Working papers and resolutions]] |
| Draft resolution | A working paper that's cleared the sponsor/signatory threshold and dais approval | [[working-papers-and-resolutions\|Working papers and resolutions]] |
| Sponsor | A resolution's author, substantively endorsing its content | [[working-papers-and-resolutions\|Working papers and resolutions]] |
| Signatory | Someone who wants a resolution debated, without endorsing its content | [[working-papers-and-resolutions\|Working papers and resolutions]] |
| Preambulatory clause | A resolution clause providing context; starts with a participle, ends in a comma | [[working-papers-and-resolutions\|Working papers and resolutions]] |
| Operative clause | A resolution clause stating what's being decided; starts with a present-tense verb | [[working-papers-and-resolutions\|Working papers and resolutions]] |
| Friendly amendment | A change every sponsor already agrees to, added without a vote | [[amendments\|Amendments]] |
| Unfriendly amendment | A contested change that goes through debate and a full committee vote | [[amendments\|Amendments]] |
| Procedural vote | A vote on process (agenda, caucuses); no abstentions allowed | [[voting-procedure\|Voting procedure]] |
| Substantive vote | A vote on content (amendments, the resolution itself); abstentions allowed | [[voting-procedure\|Voting procedure]] |
| Simple majority | More yes-votes than no-votes | [[voting-procedure\|Voting procedure]] |
| Two-thirds majority | A higher bar reserved for certain substantive decisions | [[voting-procedure\|Voting procedure]] |
| Roll call vote | A recorded vote where each delegation states its position aloud | [[voting-procedure\|Voting procedure]] |
