This wiki is built on [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/), the static site generator originally
created by [Jacky Zhao](https://jzhao.xyz/) and maintained by an open source community. The
seedling is Quartz's own mark, not ours, we borrowed it here because without Quartz there wouldn't
be a wiki to write content for.

## Software

- **[Quartz](https://github.com/jackyzha0/quartz)** – the site generator itself, MIT licensed.
- **[@quartz-community](https://github.com/orgs/quartz-community/repositories)** plugins – the
  search, graph view, dark mode, table of contents, and most of the other functionality on this
  site come from Quartz's community plugin ecosystem, not from anything custom-written here.
- **[Preact](https://preactjs.com/)** – the rendering library Quartz's components are built on.
- **[Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/)** – the typeface used across the whole site.
- **[Vercel](https://vercel.com/)** – hosting and deployment.
- **[GitHub](https://github.com/)** – source hosting and the way anyone can
  [[contributing|contribute]] back to this wiki.

## Content

The [[delegates/overview|delegate track]] was researched against established MUN institution
guides (Best Delegate, NHSMUN, THIMUN, and university conference Rules of Procedure documents)
rather than written from memory, since procedure genuinely varies conference to conference and
getting that wrong would defeat the point of the wiki.

## Design

Colors, typography, and branding here were matched to [Motion](https://motionmun.com), the
committee management app this wiki is the companion resource for. They're built with different
tools and serve different purposes, but they're meant to feel like the same project.
